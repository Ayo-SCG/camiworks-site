CAMI · Contract Administration and Management Interface

Contract administration software for government contractors, built to CPSR readiness standards.

Built by the people who run contract offices. Every date tracked, every dollar watched, every purchase documented, every action on the record.

Start a conversation

The spreadsheet doesn’t chase anyone.

Government contracts run on deadlines, funded dollars, deliverables, and documentation — and missing any one of them is expensive. Yet many contractors track all of it in spreadsheets that no one audits and no one chases. When a date slips or the funding runs down, the spreadsheet stays quiet.

What CAMI does

The master calendar

Every contractual date lives on one calendar, each with a computed internal lead and awareness of federal holidays. An escalating alert ladder defends each date: alerts require acknowledgment, and when they don’t get it, they escalate on their own.

The deliverables register

Every required submission, tied to its source requirement and a named owner. Nothing goes out without review, nothing closes without proof of delivery, and recurring deliverables regenerate themselves.

Funding and burn

The funded position for every contract and every CLIN, with invoices and payments logged against it. Burn thresholds at seventy, eighty, and ninety percent — with exhaustion projections — mean no one runs out of funded room without knowing it was coming.

Government correspondence

Every communication logged the day it happens, and nothing outbound without review. Verbal direction is confirmed in writing, and every date a letter creates lands on the calendar automatically.

The monthly status report

Assembled from the records in the system, never from memory. Reviewed, signed, and delivered on schedule — the standing proof that the contract office is running.

Purchasing built for CPSR

Requisition to purchase order with threshold-banded documentation: price analysis, fair-and-reasonable determinations, and exclusions checks. The result is a purchase file that proves itself, aligned to the 24 criteria of DFARS 252.244-7001.

How it holds up

Everything attributable

Every change in the system carries who made it, when they made it, and why.

Archive, never delete

Records are archived, never deleted. The history of a contract stays whole.

Judgment stays human

Determinations, justifications, and sign-offs are made by people, on the record. CAMI keeps the record; people make the calls.

Who it’s for

Small and midsize government contractors — primes and subcontractors alike — who need a contract office’s discipline without building a contract office. CAMI is deployed with guided onboarding and configuration by Ayo Strategic Consulting Group.
